## What I build

I build software and systems that help people make sense of complexity, navigate uncertainty, and act with better judgment.

My work sits at the intersection of:

* **Software engineering** — turning ideas into working systems.
* **Systems thinking** — understanding how people, processes, information, and technology interact.
* **Human judgment** — designing for clarity, reflection, decision quality, and accountability.
* **Responsible AI** — exploring how AI can augment human capability without removing human responsibility.

I am particularly interested in the space between **information and action** — where technology meets human reasoning.

## How I work

I tend to approach problems from first principles:

**Understand the problem → model the system → identify constraints → build → test → refine.**

I value clear architecture, useful abstractions, readable implementation, and evidence over unnecessary complexity.

The goal isn't simply to make something work.

**The goal is to make the system understandable, useful, and capable of improving.**
